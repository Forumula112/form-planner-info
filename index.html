Form Planner

Personal planning, stored on your Windows computer.

Form Planner is a desktop app for tasks, school work, projects and calendar planning. It is currently intended for the owner's personal use. This page does not offer a public download or account registration.

Google Calendar connection

You can connect your Google account to view events from your primary calendar alongside your plans. The app requests Calendar events read-only access. It cannot create, edit or delete your Google Calendar events.

The app reads a window starting today in Edmonton through the next 45 days. It refreshes while open and keeps a local cache so the last successful calendar view remains available offline. Changes made in Google Calendar appear after a successful refresh.

Google authorization opens in your browser. The app stores the resulting credentials using Windows-protected encryption. Calendar event details are cached separately on your computer and are not encrypted by the app.

You can disconnect in Settings. You can also remove access through your Google Account.
